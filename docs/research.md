# Target selection, 8 October 2026

New family: product planning. The registry has no prior build in this family. ContractSafe and procurement were excluded as already built families.

Aha! Roadmaps scores 3 annual bill, 5 data-shaped, 2 buyer fit, 3 search demand, 5 buildability and 5 import support: 23/30. The [vendor pricing page](https://www.aha.io/roadmaps/pricing) lists Enterprise at $99 per paid user/month on annual billing. Twenty paid users imply $23,760 annually before tax, an illustrative scenario, not a verified customer invoice. Enterprise includes free reviewers and viewers. Do not count them as paid seats.

No confirmed customer usage was found in local customer context. Live CRM was not queried because this charter excludes secrets; buyer fit is unverified and scored conservatively. Search results for Aha! alternatives establish competing comparison pages, not keyword volume.

Productboard was shortlisted in the same family; its current pricing page was reopened and must supersede older Pro price references. ClearPoint strategy management is quote-based and lacks verified five-figure invoice evidence. Lansweeper depends heavily on automated discovery, so an imported register is a poor substitute for its core value. Aha!'s documented CSV list export makes the scoped planning workflows more testable in one session.
